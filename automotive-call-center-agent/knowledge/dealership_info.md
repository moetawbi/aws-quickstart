# Summit Auto Group — Dealership Reference

## Locations & hours

**Summit Auto Group — Main Service Center**
1200 Summit Parkway, Springfield
- Service: Mon–Fri 7:30 AM – 6:00 PM, Sat 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM, closed Sunday
- Sales: Mon–Sat 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM, closed Sunday
- Parts counter: Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 5:30 PM

## While-you-wait & transportation

- Complimentary shuttle within 10 miles of the dealership, 8 AM – 4:30 PM weekdays.
- Loaner vehicles are available for warranty repairs expected to take more
  than 4 hours; must be reserved when booking and requires a valid license
  and insurance. Not available for routine maintenance visits.
- Comfortable waiting lounge with Wi-Fi and coffee for shorter services.

## Payment & financing

- We accept all major credit cards, debit, and cash. Personal checks up to $500.
- Service financing available on repairs over $500 through Sunbit — 3, 6, or
  12-month plans; approval takes about 5 minutes at the service desk.
- Estimates are free for visible issues; diagnostic fee ($129) applies to
  check-engine and intermittent issues, and is waived if the repair is done
  with us.

## Price & warranty policies

- Tire price match: we match any local competitor's written tire quote on
  the same brand/model/size within 30 days of purchase.
- All repairs carry a 24-month / 24,000-mile parts-and-labor warranty.
- Recall and warranty work requires no payment; bring your registration.

## Current promotions (Fall)

- Fall service special: oil change + tire rotation bundle $109 (save $20).
- Free battery and coolant health check with any scheduled service Oct–Nov.
- EV owners: complimentary Level 2 charging while your vehicle is serviced.

## Frequently asked

- **Do I need an appointment?** Walk-ins accepted for oil changes and state
  inspections before 3 PM; everything else should be scheduled.
- **Do you service all brands?** We service all makes and models; recall
  work only for the brands we sell (Honda, Ford, Toyota, Tesla-certified).
- **Can I bring my own parts?** Customer-supplied parts are installed at
  the standard labor rate but are not covered by our parts warranty.

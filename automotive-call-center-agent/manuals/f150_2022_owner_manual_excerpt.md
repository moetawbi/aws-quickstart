# 2022 Ford F-150 Owner's Manual (Excerpt)

## Wheels and tires

Recommended cold tire pressure (Lariat, 275/60R20): 35 psi front, 35 psi
rear. Check pressures monthly and before towing.

Lug nut torque specification: 150 lb-ft (204 Nm). Retorque lug nuts within
100 miles after a wheel change. Do not oil or grease wheel studs.

The tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) warning lamp illuminates when
one or more tires are 25% or more below the recommended pressure. If the
lamp flashes for about one minute and stays lit, a TPMS sensor fault is
present and the system needs service.

## Fluid capacities (3.5L EcoBoost)

- Engine oil (with filter): 6.0 quarts (5.7 L), SAE 5W-30, spec WSS-M2C961-A1
- Engine coolant: 15.0 quarts (14.2 L), Motorcraft Yellow concentrate
- Transmission fluid (10-speed 10R80): 13.1 quarts (12.4 L), Mercon ULV
- Rear axle: 5.8 pints (2.75 L), SAE 75W-85
- Fuel tank: 26.0 gallons (98.4 L); 36.0 gallons with extended range option

## Scheduled maintenance

- Every 10,000 miles or 12 months: engine oil and filter, tire rotation,
  multi-point inspection.
- Every 20,000 miles: cabin air filter.
- Every 30,000 miles: engine air filter.
- At 100,000 miles: spark plugs; inspect accessory drive belt.
- At 150,000 miles: transmission fluid and filter, transfer case fluid
  (4x4), accessory drive belt replacement, coolant change (then every
  50,000 miles).

Severe duty (frequent towing, extensive idling, off-road): halve the oil
change and transmission fluid intervals.

## Warning lamps

- **Powertrain malfunction (wrench lamp):** reduced power possible; avoid
  heavy acceleration and have the vehicle serviced promptly.
- **Check engine (steady):** emissions-related fault; safe to drive
  moderately, service soon. **Check engine (flashing):** engine misfire
  that can damage the catalytic converter - reduce speed and load
  immediately and seek service. Do not tow.
- **Brake system (red):** if it stays lit with the parking brake released,
  brake function may be impaired. Stop as soon as safely possible and do
  not continue driving. Have the vehicle towed.
- **Battery/charging:** electrical charging fault; switch off non-essential
  loads and service immediately - the vehicle may lose power.

## Towing (3.5L EcoBoost, Lariat SuperCrew 4x4)

- Maximum conventional towing: 13,000 lb with the Max Trailer Tow Package
  (11,200 lb standard); maximum tongue weight is 10% of trailer weight.
- Use Tow/Haul mode when towing above 5,000 lb.
- Trailer brakes are required above 3,000 lb in most jurisdictions; the
  factory integrated trailer brake controller supports electric and
  electric-over-hydraulic brakes.
- When towing at high ambient temperatures above 100°F, reduce gross
  combined weight by 2% per 10°F.

## Keys and remote start

Remote start runs the engine for 15 minutes (extendable once to 30). The
key fob battery is a CR2450; a "Key battery low" message appears when it
needs replacement. With a dead fob, use the mechanical key blade for the
driver door and place the fob in the backup slot inside the center console
cup holder to start.
